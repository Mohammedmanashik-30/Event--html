Fashion is more than just clothing; it is a powerful form of personal expression, culture, and lifestyle that reflects who we are. Throughout history, fashion has evolved from traditional attire rooted in heritage to fast-moving global trends shaped by media, cinema, and social influences. While it allows individuals to showcase creativity and build confidence, it also drives modern challenges like fast-fashion waste and consumer pressure. Ultimately, fashion remains a timeless canvas where art meets daily life, letting people communicate their identity without speaking a single word.
